# Design direction

Preserve the existing Manzar identity: blush pink (#e0c7cd), forest green (#2d4f35), warm white (#faf8f4), real photography, and a gentle, relational voice.

The homepage helps a visitor understand Himanshi's approach, choose a session, and book or ask a question. The workshops page helps a visitor explore conversation themes and enquire for a group. Use verified biographical details, existing fees, and genuine client reflections. Never invent qualifications, event history, outcome guarantees, availability, or statistics.

Prefer generous whitespace, expressive serif headings, quiet sans-serif controls, ruled service rows, and purposeful contrast between paper, blush, and forest sections. Avoid repeated icon cards, ambient blobs, pulsing calls to action, and delayed entry overlays. Keep primary actions visible on mobile. Every interactive element needs keyboard focus and a readable accessible name. Respect reduced motion and keep content visible before animations run.

The homepage portrait uses IMG_2542.HEIC. The Workshops gallery uses IMG_3718.HEIC and IMG_3724.HEIC. Workshop event names, dates, and descriptions are pending from the user.

Copy rules confirmed on 15 October 2026: the in-person office is in East Delhi only. Mention trauma-informed and queer-affirmative care no more than twice each in the visible homepage copy. Omit the person-centred label. Use Manzar Therapy as the brand name, without adding Space. Show the starting price only once within the hero; retain specific service fees where a visitor chooses or books a session. Keep concerns together above the service options, and keep the session selector focused on meeting format. Avoid repeating captions as adjacent headings.

Keep concerns in a distinct section immediately after the hero. The approved homepage order is Introduction, Concerns, About Himanshi, Services, Find your path, Client reflections, Workshops, FAQs, Contact. Keep the individual therapy journey together before Workshops, with Concerns and Find your path separated by About and Services. Highlight M.A. Applied Psychology, clinical specialisation, and adults aged 18–40 together near the top of the hero; do not repeat the qualification in the adjacent profile caption or the biography. Remove the lower row of all three approach cards entirely.

Use selective centering: center the mobile main headline and standalone homepage section headings, while retaining left-aligned desktop split layouts and longer reading text. Center the short hero introduction, credentials group, and primary actions as a balanced mobile composition. Keep the opening description brief so the portrait appears sooner. Preserve left alignment for service rows, form labels, and profile details.

Use IMG_0456 as a tighter, responsive crop in the About section. Keep the photograph itself unchanged and crop its presentation in the image frame. Show IMG_3718 and IMG_3724 in the Workshops gallery with their full compositions; use IMG_3718 in the homepage workshop preview.

The main IMG_2542 portrait uses a 1.35× presentation crop, anchored at 50% horizontal and 68% vertical, to bring Himanshi closer within the existing frame while keeping her full head visible. Preserve the original photograph.

Keep the statement beginning “I believe in people” beneath the About photograph without a name attribution. It was drawn from the pre-redesign About copy; its original authorship has not been verified.

Interaction refinement: retain native scrolling and mark the current About, Therapy, or Contact section in navigation. Keep the FAQ introduction alongside the answers on sufficiently tall desktop screens. Let session choices and manually selected reflections acknowledge changes with a brief, subtle fade and four-pixel movement; retain focus and cancel movement when reduced motion is requested. Keep reflection controls before the changing text so their position is stable. Use gentle, one-time About reveals, and avoid shifting service text on hover. Mobile menus must remain scrollable on short screens. Route changes start immediately at the top; same-page anchor links retain native smooth scrolling.
